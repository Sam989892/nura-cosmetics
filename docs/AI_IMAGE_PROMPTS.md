## Consistent Brand Style Notes

Apply to every generated image:
- Colour palette: warm cream (#F5EDE0), rose gold (#C9956A), deep plum (#4A1E3A), gold (#C8A25C)
- No white backgrounds — always use warm cream, marble, or rose-tinted neutrals
- Lighting: warm, soft, editorial — never harsh or clinical
- Models: South Asian skin tones (light to deep), represented across the range
- No text, watermarks, or brand logos in the image itself (added separately in design)
- Aspect ratios: 1:1 for product cards, 4:5 for PDP hero, 16:9 for campaign banners
